02 / Operational Challenges

Problems Solved by GeoConvey

1. Conveyance Claim Inflation

Manual odometer claims are routinely inflated by 20%–40%. Without verified GPS breadcrumbs, companies overpay conveyance allowances across field fleets.

2. Ghost Attendance & Proxy Check-ins

Field supervisors marking shifts without physically being on-site. Standard check-ins lack geotagged photographic proof of attendance.

3. Background GPS Disconnect

Standard web apps close GPS when the phone screen is locked or the app is minimized, causing missing travel records.

4. Manual Audit Headaches

Managers spend dozens of hours cross-referencing log sheets, paper receipts, and spreadsheets at the end of each month.

05 / Innovation

Native Background Location Tracking Service

🔔 Android Foreground Service

Runs LocationTrackingService with foregroundServiceType="location". Prevents the Android operating system from killing the GPS process.

⚡ Partial WakeLock

Holds PowerManager.PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK so the CPU does not sleep when the supervisor locks the screen and rides their bike.

📍 5-Second / 5-Meter Adaptive Filter

Accurately records GPS breadcrumbs with noise suppression (filters out points with accuracy > 50m).

📶 Offline Fallback Queue

When riding through rural network dead zones, points are stored safely in local IndexedDB/memory and batch-synced once back in range.

06 / Fraud-Proof Algorithm

Dual-Verification Distance Calculation Engine

The Lower Distance Selection Rule

To guarantee complete financial accuracy and eliminate fraud, GeoConvey implements the Lower Valid Distance Selection Algorithm:

  • • GPS Distance: Continuous geodesic summation of recorded coordinates.
  • • Odometer Distance: End KM minus Start KM with photo proof.
  • • System Decision: Automatically selects MIN(GPS_KM, Odometer_KM).
  • • Rate Application: Approved Distance × Rate/KM (e.g. ₹4.50/KM).
🛡️ Protects company if odometer is inflated; protects rider if GPS loses signal in tunnels.

Audit Case Example
